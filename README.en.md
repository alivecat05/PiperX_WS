# PiperX_WS

- [Calibration](Calibration/README.md) reads camera intrinsics, calibrates robot-base extrinsics, and verifies TCP projections.
- [HumanCapture](HumanCapture/README.en.md) records synchronized head and wrist videos of human demonstrations.
- [HumanLabel](HumanLabel/README.en.md) converts D555 videos of human demonstrations into dual-arm Piper X action CSVs.
- [PiperCapture](PiperCapture/README.en.md) records dual-arm leader–follower demonstrations and synchronized camera video.
- [PiperX_Teleop](PiperX_Teleop/README.en.md) teleoperates two Piper X arms using a Quest 3 headset.
- [PiperX_GO](PiperX_GO/README.md) deploys ACT and Diffusion Policy models for dual-arm control.
